# ClawDad 0.7 Paid Beta 2

ClawDad 0.7 beta 2 is the first founding-customer release with a signed Mac
installer, TestFlight iPhone companion, per-device pairing, StoreKit access,
and an independently verified Mac entitlement.

## Included

- Per-workspace relay credentials and one-time iPhone pairing tickets.
- Per-device relay tokens stored in the iPhone Keychain.
- Device inventory and revocation from the Mac app.
- Apple StoreKit monthly and annual plans with a 14-day introductory trial.
- Independent Apple-signed transaction verification on the paired Mac.
- Privacy-safe entitlement status shared between iPhone and Mac.
- Workspace-authorized Remote Assist signaling and ICE requests.
- Repeatable App Store Connect provisioning with a checked-in product record.
- A signed Mac updater path carried forward from beta 1.
- Durable Direct and Queue delivery through reconnects and long Codex turns.
- iPhone project and thread restoration, recent threads across projects, voice,
  images, formatted history, and explicit copy controls.
- Face ID-gated Remote Assist with landscape, keyboard, Enter, and explicit
  clipboard transfer.
- Public support and privacy pages at `clawdad-cloud.frg.earth`.

## Pricing

- Monthly: `$9.99`
- Annual: `$99.00`
- Introductory offer: 14 days free
- Founding paid beta storefront: United States

Codex or ChatGPT access is purchased separately from OpenAI.

## Beta Gates

Build 19 must complete physical TestFlight purchase, restore, cancellation,
fresh-pairing, reconnect, queue, voice, image, and Remote Assist checks. After
fresh pairing proves the per-device relay token, production relay enforcement
can be enabled.

Apple still requires the account holder to confirm the Paid Applications
Agreement, tax and banking state, app privacy questionnaire, review contact,
reviewer access, app screenshots, and the first-subscription attachment to app
version 1.0. Restrictive-network Remote Assist certification requires a
Cloudflare TURN credential with Calls Write permission.
