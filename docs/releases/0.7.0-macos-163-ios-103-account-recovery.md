# Mac 163 and iPhone 103: Account activation recovery

ClawDad now resumes an accepted activation automatically when the Mac process reader becomes available. Its original receipt and account-work hold persist across restarts, with retries spaced up to 30 seconds apart. Ambiguous delivery and ownership changes still require explicit recovery.

The iPhone distinguishes Waiting for Mac, Waiting for app work, Checking app state, and Needs attention. It shows an unavailable active-account check explicitly, refreshes the identity after reconnection, and preserves account preview and pending-request receipts. Done and the existing navigation remain available throughout.

Mac 163 includes the startup-recovery fix; iPhone 103 supplies the native presentation update. Native Mac installation and internal TestFlight are the release destinations. Ordinary Terminal authentication and sessions remain independent.
