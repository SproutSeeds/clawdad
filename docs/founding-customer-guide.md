# ClawDad Founding Customer Guide

ClawDad lets you continue the Codex projects on your Mac from your iPhone. Your
Mac remains where Codex, project files, and canonical thread history live.

## What You Need

- a Mac that can remain awake while you use ClawDad remotely
- Codex installed and signed in on that Mac
- the ClawDad Mac app
- an iPhone running iOS 18 or later
- the ClawDad TestFlight invitation
- a ClawDad subscription; OpenAI access is separate

## Install And Pair

1. Install ClawDad from the signed Mac DMG and move it to Applications.
2. Open ClawDad and choose your Scratchpad focus and project folders.
3. Install ClawDad from TestFlight on the iPhone.
4. Choose Monthly or Annual, or restore an existing purchase.
5. Open Settings on the Mac and select **Pair iPhone**.
6. Scan the fresh QR from the iPhone.
7. Choose a project and one of its Codex threads.

ClawDad reconnects automatically. The last project and thread remain selected
across app launches.

## Send Modes

**Direct** sends into the current conversation. During an active Codex turn, it
is considered after the current tool call.

**Queue** waits until the active turn is completely finished, then starts the
next turn.

## Remote Assist

Remote Assist is a manual full-screen view for the moments that need your click,
password, terminal, or browser. Enable it in Mac Settings, grant Screen
Recording and Accessibility, tap the desktop icon on iPhone, and approve Face
ID. Clipboard transfer happens only when you tap Copy or Paste.

## Help

Use **Copy Diagnostics** in Mac Settings and visit
`https://clawdad-cloud.frg.earth/support`. Diagnostics stay on your Mac until
you choose to share them.
